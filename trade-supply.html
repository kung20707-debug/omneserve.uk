Logistics

Details that matter before supply is confirmed.

Material supply can depend on access, unloading, packaging, delivery windows, and substitutions. These points are collected as inquiry information only; they are not automatic prices.

Dock, curbside, elevator, stairs, forklift availability, restricted streets, and receiving hours.

Packaging

Piece count, bundle, pallet, box, roll, bag, or full-load requirements.

Lead Time

Needed-by date, project phase, staging sequence, and acceptable partial fulfillment.

Substitution Rules

Brand-only, exact spec, equivalent accepted, budget preference, or code-related restrictions.
