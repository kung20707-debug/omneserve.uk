Material Lines

Materials that support the build and the finish.

OMNI SERVE INC. organizes construction and interior finishing materials by project phase, from structure and enclosure to drywall, flooring, tile, paint, hardware, and jobsite protection. No prices are listed on this page.

Shell & Frame

Lumber, Panels & Framing

Dimensional lumber, plywood, OSB, sheathing, blocking, framing accessories, and support items for rough construction.

  • Common uses: framing, sheathing, blocking, temporary protection.
  • Useful inquiry details: size, grade, thickness, quantity, treatment, delivery date.
Masonry & Base

Cement, Concrete & Masonry

Cement products, mortar, blocks, pavers, reinforcement accessories, patching materials, and masonry-related supplies.

  • Common uses: base work, block walls, repair, exterior hardscape.
  • Useful inquiry details: bag count, mix type, block dimensions, pallet count, jobsite access.
Envelope

Insulation & Waterproofing

Insulation boards, wraps, sealants, flashing, membranes, coatings, and moisture protection materials.

  • Common uses: exterior envelope, roof edges, wall assemblies, moisture control.
  • Useful inquiry details: R-value, roll or board size, membrane type, coverage area.
Interior Buildout

Drywall, Board & Trim

Drywall, cement board, compound, bead, trim, adhesives, tapes, and support materials for interior crews.

  • Common uses: partitions, bathrooms, fire-rated assemblies, patching, interior finish prep.
  • Useful inquiry details: board size, thickness, moisture rating, finish level, quantity.
Finish Work

Tile, Stone & Flooring Support

Tile-related materials, stone coordination, setting products, underlayment, grout, edge trims, and finish accessories.

  • Common uses: kitchens, bathrooms, lobbies, corridors, commercial finish work.
  • Useful inquiry details: square footage, color, finish, thickness, trim profile, substrate.
Site Essentials

Hardware, Fasteners & Consumables

Anchors, screws, connectors, protective supplies, blades, tapes, sealants, and repeat-purchase jobsite items.

  • Common uses: daily crew supply, punch list, installation, repair, site safety support.
  • Useful inquiry details: material type, diameter, length, pack count, application, repeat schedule.
